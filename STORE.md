# Mağaza Sayfası Metinleri

Play Console → **Ana mağaza girişi** (ve App Store Connect) için hazır metinler. Varsayılan dil İngilizce; Türkçe çeviriyi **Çeviriler → Kendi çevirilerinizi ekleyin → Türkçe** ile ekleyin. Görseller `Masaüstü\build\store` klasöründe (`tools/store_shots.gd` ile yeniden üretilebilir).

| Alan | Sınır |
|---|---|
| Uygulama adı | 30 karakter |
| Kısa açıklama | 80 karakter |
| Tam açıklama | 4000 karakter |

## English (en-US)

**App name**

```
2048 Olympos: Gods & Merge
```

**Short description**

```
Classic 2048 with Greek gods: Zeus doubles tiles, and Chaos awaits at 2048!
```

**Full description**

```
Slide, merge and reach 2048 — but you are not alone. The gods of Olympos are watching.

2048 Olympos is the classic number puzzle with a mythological twist, drawn in crisp 8-bit pixel art with chiptune sound.

⚡ ZEUS strikes a tile with lightning and doubles it. Rarely his bolt chains from tile to tile. When you are stuck, he may save you once.

💘 EROS merges every pair of equal neighbors at once, and sometimes gifts you a Love Potion: lift any tile off the board and put it back wherever you like.

🔥 ARES lets out a war cry that blows every 2 off the board, then multiplies your score for a few moves.

👁 THE HERALD arrives at 512: a mini boss sent by Chaos. Every merge damages him.

🌀 CHAOS awakens at 2048. The gods vanish, the sky turns red and the music changes. Merge tiles to bring down his health bar while he halves your tiles, splits them apart and floods the board with 2s. Each fight he has a different mood. Beat him and the gods return!

FEATURES
• The classic 2048 rules you know, with three gods who help you
• Two boss fights with their own music
• Trophies to collect and a record for beating Chaos
• Your game is saved; continue any time
• Short guides explain everything the first time
• English and Turkish
• Plays offline
• No pay-to-win: no purchases, no loot boxes

Rewarded ads are always optional: watch one to continue a lost game or to get a Love Potion.

Made by a solo developer with Godot Engine.
```
